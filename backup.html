💾 Backup & restore

Download my backup

Saves everything in your PeopleLink into one file: people, groups, notes, journal, reminders, family links, households and activities. Keep it somewhere safe, like your computer or a cloud drive.

Unlike exports, a backup includes your private notes and journal — so keep the file private too.

Restore from a backup

Restoring replaces everything in your PeopleLink with what's in the file. Anything added since that backup is lost. Download a fresh backup first if you're not sure.
