# 🤝 PeopleLink

*More than contacts. It's about connections.*

A private, simple way to keep track of the people in your life — names,
phones, emails, addresses, birthdays, photos and notes — organized into as
many groups as you like. Part of the MyDataWorld family of apps; open it
from My Apps Hub.

**Status:** v0.1, Milestone A (people, groups, search, photos, Help, About).
Coming next: Google Contacts CSV import/export and group sharing
(Milestone B), then journal, reminders, relationships and activities
(Milestone C).

See [SETUP.md](SETUP.md) to deploy.
