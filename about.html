Our mission

People matter, and relationships deserve attention.

Life introduces us to family, friends, neighbors, coworkers, and others who become part of our journey. Our mission is to provide a simple, thoughtful way to organize the people in our lives, remember the things that matter to them, and nurture meaningful relationships.

PeopleLink goes beyond names, addresses, and phone numbers. It helps us remember conversations, celebrate milestones, stay connected, and make time for the people who enrich our lives.

Because the most important things we keep track of aren't things at all. They're people.

What it does

  • Keeps everyone in one private place — names, phones, emails, addresses, birthdays, photos and notes.
  • Organizes people into as many groups as you like.
  • Finds anyone fast by name, number, email, place or group.
  • Reminds you of birthdays and anniversaries coming up.
  • One tap to call, text, email, map, or add a birthday to your calendar.
