Getting started

  1. Open PeopleLink from My Apps Hub — it signs you in automatically.
  2. Tap People, then + Add person.
  3. Type a name. Everything else is optional — add a phone, email, address, birthday or notes if you have them.
  4. Tap Save. You'll see their page, with buttons to call, text, or email them.

Everything you enter is private to your login. Other people using PeopleLink can't see your list.

Managing people

  1. Find someone: on People, type in the search box. It searches names, nicknames, phone numbers (with or without dashes), emails, addresses and group names.
  2. Filter: pick a group, choose Active / Archived / Deceased / Everyone, or tick ⭐ Pinned or 🎂 This month.
  3. Edit: open the person and tap ✏️ Edit. Use + Add phone, + Add email or + Add address for more than one; the ✕ removes one.
  4. Photo: in Edit, tap 📷 Add photo. On a phone you can take a picture or choose one. It's trimmed to a square to fit the round picture.
  5. Birthday without a year: leave the year blank — reminders still work, the age just isn't shown.
  6. Pin: tap the ☆ next to their name to keep them on your Dashboard. Tap ★ to unpin.
  7. Add a birthday to your calendar: tap 📅 next to the birthday or anniversary on their page.
  8. Map an address: tap 🗺️ Map next to it.

Archive, deceased, and delete

  • Archive hides someone from your lists and Dashboard without losing anything. Choose Archived in the People filter to see them, and Make active to bring them back.
  • Mark deceased keeps their record and memories but stops birthday reminders.
  • Delete removes the person and everything about them for good. It asks first.

Groups

  1. Tap Groups, then + New group. Give it a name, and if you like a color and an icon.
  2. Open the group and tap + Add people to tick everyone who belongs.
  3. Or, on a person's page, tap Change under Groups.
  4. A person can be in as many groups as you like.
  5. The ✕ next to someone in a group only takes them out of that group — it never deletes them.
  6. Archive a group you don't use any more to hide it; Delete group removes just the group, never the people in it.

Doing many at once

  1. On People, tap Select.
  2. Tick the people you want (or Select all shown after searching or filtering).
  3. Choose a group and tap Add to group or Remove from group — or use Set status… to archive them.
  4. Tap Done when you're finished.

The Dashboard

  • ⭐ Pinned shows the people you pinned.
  • 🎉 Next 30 days lists birthdays and anniversaries coming up, with how old they're turning when you know the year.
  • Groups shows each group and how many people are in it — tap one to open it.

Privacy

Your people, groups, notes and photos belong to your login only. Notes are never shared with anyone and won't be included in exports.
