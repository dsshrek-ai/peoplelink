Getting started

  1. Open PeopleLink from My Apps Hub — it signs you in automatically.
  2. Tap People, then + Add person.
  3. Type a name. Everything else is optional — add a phone, email, address, birthday or notes if you have them.
  4. Tap Save. You'll see their page, with buttons to call, text, or email them.

Everything you enter is private to your login. Other people using PeopleLink can't see your list.

Managing people

  1. Bring in your Google contacts: see Import from Google Contacts below.
  2. Find someone: on People, type in the search box. It searches names, nicknames, phone numbers (with or without dashes), emails, addresses and group names.
  3. Filter: pick a group, choose Active / Archived / Deceased / Everyone, or tick ⭐ Pinned or 🎂 This month.
  4. Edit: open the person and tap ✏️ Edit. Use + Add phone, + Add email or + Add address for more than one; the ✕ removes one.
  5. Photo: in Edit, tap 📷 Add photo. On a phone you can take a picture or choose one. It's trimmed to a square to fit the round picture.
  6. Birthday without a year: leave the year blank — reminders still work, the age just isn't shown.
  7. Pin: tap the ☆ next to their name to keep them on your Dashboard. Tap ★ to unpin.
  8. Add a birthday to your calendar: tap 📅 next to the birthday or anniversary on their page.
  9. Map an address: tap 🗺️ Map next to it.

Archive, deceased, and delete

  • Archive hides someone from your lists and Dashboard without losing anything. Choose Archived in the People filter to see them, and Make active to bring them back.
  • Mark deceased keeps their record and memories but stops birthday reminders.
  • Delete removes the person and everything about them for good. It asks first.

Groups

  1. Tap Groups, then + New group. Give it a name, and if you like a color and an icon.
  2. Open the group and tap + Add people to tick everyone who belongs.
  3. Or, on a person's page, tap Change under Groups.
  4. A person can be in as many groups as you like.
  5. The ✕ next to someone in a group only takes them out of that group — it never deletes them.
  6. Archive a group you don't use any more to hide it; Delete group removes just the group, never the people in it.

Doing many at once

  1. On People, tap Select.
  2. Tick the people you want (or Select all shown after searching or filtering).
  3. Choose a group and tap Add to group or Remove from group — or use Set status… to archive them, Stay in touch… to set the same reminder for all of them, ✉️ Message, Export, or Delete.
  4. Tap Done when you're finished.

The Dashboard

  • ⭐ Pinned shows the people you pinned.
  • 🎉 Next 30 days lists birthdays and anniversaries coming up, with how old they're turning when you know the year.
  • Groups shows each group and how many people are in it — tap one to open it.

Reports

  1. Tap Reports (under More on a phone) and pick one:
    • 🎂 Birthdays & anniversaries — the next 30/60/90 days, or the whole year by month.
    • ⏰ Upcoming contacts — who's overdue or due soon.
    • 🤫 Haven't talked to lately — no contact logged in 3, 6 or 12 months.
    • 📒 Group directory — a roster with phones, emails and addresses.
    • 💌 Card & mailing list — one address per household, ready for cards or labels.
    • 📈 Contact summary — how often you've been in touch each month, and with whom.
  2. Use the choices at the top (like a group) to narrow it down.
  3. Tap 🖨️ Print, or ⬇️ CSV to open it in Excel or Google Sheets (for example, to print mailing labels).

Duplicates

  1. On People, tap Find duplicates (also under More).
  2. Pairs that share an email, phone number or name are listed.
  3. Tap Merge…, choose which one stays, and tap Merge. Everything from the other one moves over — numbers, addresses, groups, notes, journal, activities, family links — and then it's removed.
  4. If two people really are different, tap Not the same to hide that pair.

Backup & restore

  1. Go to More → 💾 Backup & restore and tap Download backup. Tick Include photos to keep pictures too.
  2. Save the file somewhere safe. It includes your private notes and journal, so keep it private.
  3. To restore, choose the file, check the summary, type REPLACE, and tap Restore this backup. This replaces everything currently in PeopleLink with the backup.

A good habit: download a backup every month or two, and always before a big import.

Privacy

Your people, groups, notes and photos belong to your login only. Notes are never shared with anyone and won't be included in exports.

Import from Google Contacts

  1. On a computer, open contacts.google.com. Tick the contacts you want (or choose a label on the left).
  2. Choose Export → Google CSV and save the file.
  3. In PeopleLink, go to People → ⬆️ Import and choose that file.
  4. Groups: tick the Google labels you want as PeopleLink groups. Google's automatic "Imported on…" labels start unticked. Starred contacts can be pinned to your Dashboard.
  5. Organizations: if several contacts share an organization (for example, a choir saved as the "Organization"), you can tick it to make a group with that name too.
  6. Duplicates: anyone who looks like a person you already have (same email, phone, or name) is listed. For each, choose Merge, Add as new, or Skip.
  7. Tap Import. You'll see how many were added, merged, and skipped.

Merge never overwrites. It only fills in blanks and adds phone numbers, emails, and addresses the person doesn't already have. Your notes stay exactly as they were.

Nothing is saved until the last step — you can cancel any time before that. Importing the same file twice is safe: everyone already added shows up as a duplicate you can skip.

Export

  1. Go to People → ⬇️ Export, or open a group and tap ⬇️ Export, or tap Select on People, tick some people, and tap Export.
  2. Choose Google Contacts CSV (to import at contacts.google.com: Import → choose the file) or Standard CSV (to open in Excel or Google Sheets).
  3. When exporting one group, choose whether each person's other groups come along as labels too.
  4. Check the preview and tap Download.

Exports never include notes, photos, or pins. Google accepts up to 3,000 contacts per file, so bigger exports download as several files.

Sharing a group

  1. Open the group and tap 📤 Share.
  2. Check the list of who will be shared, and tick Include profile photos if you want.
  3. Type the other person's MyDataWorld username and tap Send invitation. (They need PeopleLink access in My Apps Hub.)

When someone shares with you: the Dashboard says a shared group is waiting. Tap Review, choose a new group or one of yours to put everyone in, pick Merge, Add, or Skip for each person, and tap Accept — or Decline.

What's shared: names, nicknames, phones, emails, addresses, the group's name and description, and photos if chosen. Never shared: notes, birthdays, anniversaries, work details, pins, or the other groups people are in.

It's a copy, not a link — changes either of you make afterwards aren't passed along. You can cancel an invitation from Groups → 📨 Invitations until it's accepted.

Notes & Journal

  • Notes (on ✏️ Edit) are for things that don't change much — kids' names, favorite foods, how you met.
  • The Journal is a dated record of each time you're in touch.
  1. On someone's page, tap 📝 Log contact.
  2. Check the date, choose how (call, text, email, visit, activity, other), and jot down what you talked about.
  3. If you need to get back to them, set Follow up by.
  4. Tap 📷 Add photos to keep pictures with the entry.
  5. Tap Save. It shows on their Journal tab and on the Journal page (under More on a phone).

Tap Edit on any entry to change or delete it. Journals are private — never shared or exported.

Reminders: staying in touch

  1. On someone's page (Info tab), find ⏰ Stay in touch.
  2. Under Get in touch, choose how often — every week, month, 3 months, year, or Custom… for any number of days. Tap Save.
  3. Each time you 📝 Log contact, the clock starts over.
  4. For a one-time reminder, set Follow up by a date instead (or as well). Logging a contact clears it.

Many at once: on a group page, use Stay in touch with everyone; or on People, tap Select, tick people, and choose Stay in touch….

Everyone starts on N/A — no reminders, never overdue. Reminders (under More on a phone) lists who's overdue, due this week, and coming up, and the Dashboard shows the most urgent. On People, the contact status filter finds who's overdue or who you haven't talked to in a year.

Family, friends & households

  1. On someone's page, open the Family & friends tab.
  2. Choose a person and how they're related (for example, "Tom is Margaret's child"), then tap Add.
  3. The link appears on both pages — Margaret shows on Tom's page as his parent.

Households: on the Info tab, under 🏡 Household, tap + New household (it starts with this person's address) or join an existing one. The household page has the shared address and home phone, and you can add everyone who lives there. Each person keeps their own phones and emails.

Activities

  1. Tap Activities, then + Plan activity (or 📅 Plan activity on a group to invite the whole group).
  2. Give it a title and date; a time and place are optional.
  3. Tap + Add people, or pick + Add a whole group.
  4. Save. From the activity you can 📅 Add to calendar, 🗺️ Map the place, or ✉️ Message everyone.
  5. Afterwards, tap ✅ Mark completed. Leave Add it to each person's journal ticked and it's logged for everyone (and counts as being in touch).

Past activities that weren't marked done show under Did these happen?

Email or text a group

  1. Open a group and tap ✉️ Message — or on People tap Select, tick people, and tap ✉️ Message.
  2. Choose ✉️ Email or 💬 Text, and untick anyone you want to leave out.
  3. Email: leave Use BCC ticked so people don't see each other's addresses. Tap Open email.
  4. Text: tap Open Messages. This starts a group text, so everyone sees everyone's replies. For more than 20 people it offers batches.

The message is sent from your own phone or email app. For a very long list, use 📋 Copy and paste the addresses yourself.
